# ToS / ethics notes (x-bookmark-export)

- One owner's bookmarks, saved by that owner's connected X account. A bookmark is interest, not an endorsement, and not permission to repost.
- No cookies, tokens, passwords, or browser profiles. No `api.x.com`. No undocumented X endpoints.
- Ego Lite is not invoked. It is not a verified backend for this skill. Do not describe a green unit test as an Ego smoke.
- The script does not remove bookmarks and does not download media.
- Examples use `/tmp/x-bookmark-archive` and synthetic ids. Do not point `--archive` at a private export copied from another machine.
- Do not commit archive directories, receipt keys, or page files that contain real posts.
